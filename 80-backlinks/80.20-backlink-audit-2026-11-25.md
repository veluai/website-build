## Spam campaign

Ninety-one of the 111 new-domain rows are `pattern_spam`. Sixty-three
current referring domains use the source path
`/dir/outreach-link-building-224659` and the anchor `increase google
visibility with high quality backlinks velucpa.com`. All 63 are follow.
Their highest domain authority in this export is 30, and the median spam
score is about 43.

The new list contains 84 rows with that same path and anchor. Some of those
are no longer in the current 230. The candidate file has 92 domains: the 91
`pattern_spam` rows from the new list, plus one outreach-path domain that is
in the current list but not in the new list. Seven of the 91 are a related
pitch (`high-quality dofollow link building...`) on paths such as
`/all/857/13.html`, not the outreach path. The file is
[80.50](80.50-disavow-candidates-2026-11-25.csv).

Another 34 current `pattern_spam` rows use other anchors (private-network
pitches, `velucpa.com`, and similar). They are in the current snapshot and
are not repeated into the candidate file.

Google generally ignores this kind of mass directory and network linking.
Disavow is optional. It was not uploaded.

## Chart of accounts, Blackbaud, and Nonprofit Hub

Blackbaud's blog
`https://blog.blackbaud.com/statement-of-activities-common-mistakes-and-how-to-avoid-them/`
(DA 69, nofollow) points at
`https://velu.us/resources/how-to-implement-a-chart-of-accounts/`
with the anchor `unified chart of accounts for nonprofits ucoa`. That URL
returns 404. publicnow.com (DA 62, follow) and four weaker domains use the
same target and anchor.

Nonprofit Hub's article
`https://nonprofithub.org/how-to-implement-a-chart-of-accounts-for-nonprofits/`
(DA 52, follow) does not point at `/chart-of-accounts/`. It points at
`https://velu.us/home` with the anchor `what is a chart of accounts`. `/home`
already 301s to the homepage, so the link resolves. The anchor promises a
chart-of-accounts page, and the visitor gets Home.

There is no live chart-of-accounts guide. The URL was not redirected to the
homepage. Restoring the guide is the recommendation. Outreach to Blackbaud
and Nonprofit Hub waits on a real URL to give them.

The QuickBooks export image is the same missing how-to, not a service page.
It stays a restoration item. GoHighLevel also will not redirect that
`/wp-content/uploads/` path. See the playbook.
