## What this says

The live service pages are not what the export links to. Outsourced Accounting
has one nofollow domain. Client Advisory, Grant Advisory, Nonprofit Tax
Services, About, and Get Started have none in this snapshot.

The links that name a subject point at guides and template files from the old
WordPress site. The chart-of-accounts guide is the only one with a named
publisher (Blackbaud) rather than a template host. The homepage collects the
directory links and almost all of the spam.

This is why [BL-02](80.62-bl-02-restore-chart-of-accounts-guide.md) is a
content restoration and not another homepage redirect.
