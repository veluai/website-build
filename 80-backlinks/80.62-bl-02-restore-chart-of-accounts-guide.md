# BL-02 — Restore the chart-of-accounts guide

- ID: BL-02
- Status: Open
- Owner: Tyler Wilcox
- Effort: Large. This is a new or restored page, not a redirect.
- Expected impact: Six current referring domains, including Blackbaud (DA 69, nofollow) and publicnow.com (DA 62, follow), point at a URL that returns 404. Peer firms in [80.29](80.29-competitor-link-gap-2026-11-25.md) rank explainers on this kind of subject. Velu's overview shows 7 organic keywords and 24 search traffic. Restoring this guide is the content Velu already has links for.

## Rationale

`https://velu.us/resources/how-to-implement-a-chart-of-accounts/` returned 404 on November 25. There is no live page that is that guide. It was not redirected to the homepage.

The QuickBooks export image is the same missing how-to and also 404s. GoHighLevel will not redirect that `/wp-content/uploads/` path. A restored guide is the useful destination. Nonprofit Hub already links to `/home` with the anchor "what is a chart of accounts." That link resolves. It is the wrong page for the anchor, which [BL-03](80.63-bl-03-update-chart-of-accounts-links.md) handles after a URL exists.

## Acceptance criteria

- A live URL on velu.us is the chart-of-accounts guide, in Velu's voice, without new services, prices, or credentials.
- The old guide URL 301s to that URL, one hop, then 200. It does not 301 to the homepage.
- The linking domains are listed in the log.
- BL-03 stays open until those publishers are asked.

## Out of scope

Do not start a blog program in this item. Do not redirect the export image to a service page.
