# BL-03 — Ask publishers to update chart-of-accounts links

- ID: BL-03
- Status: Open
- Owner: Tyler Wilcox
- Effort: Small once BL-02 has a URL. Two notes, not a campaign.
- Expected impact: Blackbaud's article and Nonprofit Hub's article stop pointing at a dead guide and at the homepage. publicnow.com repeats the same anchor and may not have an editor to contact.

## Rationale

Depends on [BL-02](80.62-bl-02-restore-chart-of-accounts-guide.md). Until that URL exists, there is nothing honest to ask them to use.

- Blackbaud: `https://blog.blackbaud.com/statement-of-activities-common-mistakes-and-how-to-avoid-them/` links to the dead guide. Nofollow, DA 69.
- Nonprofit Hub: `https://nonprofithub.org/how-to-implement-a-chart-of-accounts-for-nonprofits/` links to `https://velu.us/home` with the anchor "what is a chart of accounts." Follow, DA 52. `/home` already 301s to the homepage.
- publicnow.com, DA 62, follow, uses the same guide URL and anchor. Treat it as a copy. Do not make it a separate project unless a contact is obvious.

## Acceptance criteria

- BL-02 is logged as done and the new URL returns 200.
- Blackbaud and Nonprofit Hub have been asked to use that URL, or the attempt and the response are logged.
- Nobody was asked to link to the homepage.

## Out of scope

Do not email the template-farm domains on the image URLs.
