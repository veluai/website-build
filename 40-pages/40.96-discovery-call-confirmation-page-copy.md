## Visible copy

### Your discovery call

A conversation about your nonprofit, the financial challenges you’re facing, and what you want to improve.

**45 minutes · Microsoft Teams**

### Check your session details

Check your confirmation email or calendar invitation for the date, time, time
zone, and Microsoft Teams joining link. Add the appointment to your calendar and
check that the time zone is right for you.

### What our conversation will be like

We’ll talk about your organization, your priorities, and where clearer financial information or support could help. You’ll have time to ask questions and explore whether Velu is the right fit. There is no obligation.

### Before we meet

1. Choose the one or two financial challenges you most want to discuss.
2. Think about what better support would help your organization achieve.
3. Note any important reporting, funding, or decision deadlines.

No presentation or document upload is needed for this conversation. Bring your questions and a sense of what matters most.

### Need to change your plans?

Use the reschedule or cancel option in your confirmation, if provided. If you
need help or cannot find your confirmation, check your spam folder, then email
us at info@velu.us. Please avoid booking again just to request help.

Haven’t scheduled yet? [Return to Discovery Call booking](https://velu.us/get-started).

## Implementation notes

Use the title as the page's only H1; the four section headings are H2s. The HTML
asset contains this copy with scoped styles, no scripts, and protected plain-text
body email markup. The facts shown here are session type, duration, and meeting
method, not an individual appointment record. Confirm duration/method against
current calendar settings before publication. The provider's booking-success
screen is the authoritative success signal; this static page does not look up or
certify a booking. Do not accept appointment details or customer data from URL
parameters. If native verified appointment context is available later, assess it
separately before adding personalized details.
