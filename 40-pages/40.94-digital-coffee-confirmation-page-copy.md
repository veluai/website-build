## Visible copy

### Your digital coffee

A relaxed conversation with Tyler J. Wilcox, CPA to connect, exchange ideas, and hear what’s on your mind as a nonprofit leader.

**15 minutes · Microsoft Teams**

### Check your session details

Check your confirmation email or calendar invitation for the date, time, time
zone, and Microsoft Teams joining link. Add the appointment to your calendar and
check that the time zone is right for you.

### What our conversation will be like

We’ll make introductions and talk about a topic or question you’d like to explore. This is a short, informal conversation; you do not need to prepare a presentation or be looking for accounting services.

### Before we meet

1. Pick one question, idea, or experience you’d like to share.
2. Be ready to introduce yourself and your organization briefly.
3. Bring your coffee—or whatever you enjoy drinking.

No homework or documents needed. Just bring yourself and a topic you care about.

### Need to change your plans?

Use the reschedule or cancel option in your confirmation, if provided. If you
need help or cannot find your confirmation, check your spam folder, then email
us at info@velu.us. Please avoid booking again just to request help.

Haven’t scheduled yet? [Return to Digital Coffee booking](https://velu.us/digital-coffee).
