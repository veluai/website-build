# Current site index

This is the documented live surface of velu.us. The original nine-page set is
supported by the October 30 records; three appointment pages were published and
page-level live-verified November 15, 2026. Evidence and remaining runtime limits
are in [70.30](../70-implementation/70.30-appointment-pages-implementation.md).
There are twelve canonical pages; `/home` remains an alias, not another page.
Only ten indexable pages are selected for the sitemap: the two confirmation
utilities intentionally use noindex. A live page is not a verified appointment.

The approved URL form is `https://velu.us/` for Home and no trailing slash on
every other page. On November 20, 2026, about 3:50–4:01 PM CST, `/home` and
`/home/` were live-verified as one 301 to `https://velu.us/`. The HighLevel
Home path is `/velu-home` (page name `home`; still the homepage).
`https://velu.us/velu-home` returns 200 with that homepage and canonical
`https://velu.us/`. It is unlinked and not a sitemap URL. The dated check list
is
[70.40-canonical-url-consolidation.md](../70-implementation/70.40-canonical-url-consolidation.md).

On November 22, 2026, about 6:47 PM Central, the Client Advisory page
published a FAQ section and `FAQPage` schema on its existing URL. The check
is in [the changelog](../10-operations/10.20-changelog.md).
