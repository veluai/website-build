# Case Study Summary

Internal source record for a potential anonymized case study. The central factual story is that a small behavioral health practice moved from confusing, behind QuickBooks records to clean, current, reconciled, tax-ready books and a usable monthly reporting package.

## Client Background

Small, owner-operated for-profit counseling and behavioral health business. Do not identify the actual client publicly.

## Starting Situation / Challenge

- The books required approximately 15 months of cleanup and catch-up work covering 2025 through Q1 2026.
- The 2024 tax filing was also behind.
- The owner had little confidence in the financial information.
- QuickBooks Online contained seven connected bank accounts; six were personal or otherwise not legitimate business accounts.
- Personal and business transactions were mixed, and bank reconciliations were not current.
- Inventory and other asset accounts carried balances that were not applicable to the business.
- Two loan accounts showed negative balances even though they were not legitimate business loans.
- Equity accounts mixed personal and business activity and were not properly organized.
- The chart of accounts combined the standard QuickBooks structure with client-created customizations and was difficult to interpret.
- Financial statements did not clearly tell the financial story of the business.

## What Velu Did

- Analyzed the existing QuickBooks Online file and developed a corrective action plan.
- Coordinated with the owner to understand questionable transactions and agree on corrections.
- Completed approximately 15 months of catch-up bookkeeping.
- Reconciled business accounts and corrected historical balances.
- Removed or properly accounted for personal activity.
- Cleaned up invalid asset, liability, loan, and equity accounts.
- Redesigned the chart of accounts.
- Separated different therapy and service revenue streams.
- Reorganized expenses into clearer cost-of-services, marketing, and administrative categories.
- Completed adjusting entries and month-end accounting.
- Developed a monthly reporting package.
- Helped the owner better distinguish business activity from personal activity.
- Delivered clean, current, tax-ready books to the client's tax preparer.

## Accounting / Process Improvements

- Business accounts were reconciled and separated from personal activity.
- Invalid or inapplicable asset, liability, loan, and equity balances were cleaned up or properly accounted for.
- The chart of accounts was rebuilt to improve interpretability.
- Revenue streams and expense categories were organized to make the operating picture clearer.
- A repeatable month-end accounting and reporting approach was established for the cleanup period.

## Reporting and Deliverables

- Profit & Loss
- Balance Sheet
- Statement of Cash Flows
- Accounts Receivable Aging Summary
- Clean, current, tax-ready books for the client's tax preparer

## Outcomes

- Books and reconciliations became current.
- Business accounting was separated from personal transactions.
- Historical balances were cleaned up.
- The client received a clear, organized chart of accounts.
- Financial statements became understandable and useful for managing the business.
- Books were tax-ready for the tax preparer.
- The owner had a single monthly reporting package and greater confidence in the financial information.
- The client elected to maintain ongoing bookkeeping internally after the cleanup.
- The client expressed strong appreciation and indicated willingness to recommend Velu.
