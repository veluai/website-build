# Case Study Summary

Internal source record for a public nonprofit case study. The central factual story is that Nebraska Communities United moved from manual ledgers and Excel to a structured, cloud-based accounting and reporting function with monthly accounting, bill pay, controls, and board-ready reporting.

## Client Background

Nebraska Communities United (NCU) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit focused on protecting water, land, and air resources; rural economic well-being; regenerative agriculture; and rural quality of life.

## Starting Situation / Challenge

- NCU had existed as a startup-stage nonprofit for several years.
- Accounting was maintained manually through ledger books and Excel.
- Existing transactions needed to be brought current for the current year.
- The organization needed a structured nonprofit accounting system.
- Monthly accounting, reconciliation, reporting, bill approval/payment, and internal-control practices needed to be established.

## What Velu Did

- Set up QuickBooks Online for nonprofit accounting.
- Designed a nonprofit-specific chart of accounts.
- Established program and class tracking.
- Recorded opening balances and brought current-year accounting activity up to date.
- Connected bank feeds and established monthly reconciliations.
- Implemented a cloud-based bill approval and payment system and integrated it with QuickBooks Online.
- Established bill approval and payment workflows.
- Set up 1099 tracking and related accounting processes.
- Established treasurer review of monthly bank reconciliations.
- Created a recurring financial reporting package for management and the board.
- Trained and supported the client on bill approvals, payments, and reconciliation-review responsibilities.
- Continued providing ongoing monthly accounting and bill pay after implementation.

## Accounting / Process Improvements

- Nonprofit-specific chart of accounts and program/class tracking established.
- Monthly bank reconciliations and treasurer review implemented.
- Separation of accounting, reconciliation review, bill approval, and payment responsibilities established.
- Cloud-based bill approval and payment capability made remote review and approval possible.
- Recurring monthly accounting, bill pay, and management/board reporting processes established.

## Reporting and Deliverables

- Statement of Financial Position
- Statement of Activities
- Statement of Cash Flows
- Accounts Receivable Aging Summary
- Recurring financial reporting package for management and board review

## Outcomes

- NCU moved from ledger books and Excel to a functioning cloud-based accounting system.
- QuickBooks Online was customized around nonprofit reporting needs.
- The client had regular monthly reconciliations and a consistent reporting package for board review.
- Bill approval and payment became cloud-based and accessible remotely.
- Internal controls and separation of duties improved.
- Velu continued as the ongoing monthly accounting and bill pay partner.
