# Case Study Summary

Internal source record for a public case study. The central factual story is that Velu became the long-term outsourced accounting function for two related businesses, providing disciplined monthly close, timely reporting, accounts-receivable and project visibility, stronger controls, and nearly seven years of recurring support.

## Client Background

GC Resolve and GC ReVOLT are two related Nebraska businesses under the same owner.

GC Resolve was founded in 2015 and is a consulting and communications company focused on environmental and equity issues, including regenerative agriculture, agricultural competition and concentration, agricultural markets, rural socioeconomic issues, agricultural workforce inequality, land transition, water and air quality, and related topics. Approximate annual size discussed: $145,000.

GC ReVOLT is a solar and alternative-energy development company focused on renewable-energy education and development in Nebraska and the Heartland. Its work includes residential, farm, commercial, community, and larger renewable-energy development. Approximate annual size discussed: $800,000.

## Starting Situation / Challenge

- Accounting was already maintained in QuickBooks Online.
- Internal operations staff, rather than dedicated accounting personnel, were handling the accounting.
- The existing process was functional but could be more structured.
- Opportunities existed to improve month-end close, consistency and timeliness of financial reporting, accounts receivable management, project invoicing visibility, chart-of-accounts design, bill payment processes, credit-card receipt/documentation practices, internal controls, and ongoing tax readiness.

## What Velu Did

- Performed a modest accounting cleanup.
- Redesigned the chart of accounts around appropriate revenue, cost-of-goods-sold, and operating-expense categories.
- Established proper bill-pay processes.
- Implemented a cloud-based bill approval and payment system.
- Improved accounting workflows and controls.
- Helped establish spend-management practices, including credit-card expense documentation.
- Provided ongoing bookkeeping and transaction accounting for both businesses.
- Performed bank, credit-card, and balance-sheet reconciliations.
- Completed month-end review of the balance sheet and profit and loss, with adjusting/accounting entries as needed.
- Managed bill pay, customer invoicing, accounts receivable monitoring, and tracking of outstanding customer invoices and payments.
- Held periodic owner meetings covering accounting and tax matters.

## Accounting / Process Improvements

- A more disciplined recurring month-end close was established.
- The chart of accounts was redesigned for clearer revenue, cost-of-goods-sold, and operating-expense reporting.
- Reconciliations, approval workflows, separation of duties, and documented close procedures strengthened the accounting process.
- Spend-management and credit-card documentation practices improved.
- Cloud-based bill approval and payment supported clearer workflow and controls.
- GC ReVOLT gained a progress-invoicing process for larger solar and construction projects.

## Reporting and Deliverables

- Balance Sheet
- Profit & Loss
- Statement of Cash Flows
- GC ReVOLT Progress Invoicing Report
- Financial statements completed by the 10th of the following month or sooner

## Outcomes

- Consistent monthly close and timely, reliable financial statements.
- Better visibility into customer invoicing and collections.
- Better tracking of solar-project estimates, invoices, and payments.
- Stronger credit-card documentation and spend controls.
- Stronger approval workflows and separation of duties.
- Businesses consistently maintained for tax readiness.
- Owner freed to focus on operating and growing the companies.
- Long-term relationship continued for nearly seven years.
